School and College Enrollment Validation

Does PopulationSim’s synthetic population match real K-12 and college enrollment?

Purpose

This site checks the PopulationSim synthetic population behind WFRC’s travel demand model against real school and college enrollment. The study area is Box Elder, Davis, Weber, Salt Lake, and Utah counties. K-12 enrollment is compared by school district. College enrollment is compared by county.

PopulationSim does not control on school enrollment, so each comparison runs two ways:

  • Enrollment status (the real test). The synthetic population’s school-grade label (SCHG) is uncontrolled, so it is compared directly with actual enrollment.
  • Age population (a sanity bound). Age is controlled in PopulationSim. The population in the school-age band should meet or exceed enrollment. It is not expected to match exactly.

Pages

Page What it does Status
1. Geography Builds the school-to-district and college-to-county crosswalks. MAZ rows in both let PopulationSim output roll up. Checks coverage and maps the study area. Done
2. Enrollment Real K-12 enrollment by district (Utah DOE, NCES CCD, NCES private) and college enrollment by county (USHE, IPEDS). Compares the sources. Done
3. Synthetic population Rolls PopulationSim persons up to district and county by school grade and by age. Done, except the Box Elder clip
4. Validation Real against synthetic, by district and by county: tables, scatter plots, and level charts. Districts and counties done; Box Elder and the summary are still to do

How the site is built

The pages run in order. Each one writes CSV and GeoJSON files to _data/intermediate/, and the next page reads them. The charts are built in Observable JavaScript from those files, so they update on every render.

Known limitations

  • Box Elder is only partly inside the model region. Synthetic persons exist only inside model MAZs, so Box Elder needs clipping to the model boundary before it can be compared. That clip is still a TODO.
  • USHE and IPEDS are not like-for-like. USHE covers public institutions only and is Academic Year
    1. IPEDS includes private institutions and is fall 2023. The county validation has an option to include private institutions.
  • Residence against location. Synthetic college counts are where students live. IPEDS counts by institution location, and USHE counts by site.
  • Charter schools are included in the district totals. Whether Utah DOE credits their enrollment to the resident district has not been checked against DOE’s published totals.
  • Online-only schools have no geographic catchment, so they are left out of the district totals and reported separately.
  • Private schools (NCES Private School Universe Survey) appear on the enrollment page but not in the district validation table.

Code, setup, and how to re-render the site are in the project README.
